# How to play
**Controls:** 
* mouse movement
* click

**Objective:** 
* Prepare coffe
* Serve customers

**Gameplay:** <img align = "right" width="459.5" height="257" alt="20260803-1749-03 5179592" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b6acedce-a685-4ee5-a22f-9f1d169865d7" />

* open the kitchen panel
* click the empty coffe cup
* fill the coffe cups with the coffe maker
* drag the full coffe cups to the box
* serve the customers
* upgrade
